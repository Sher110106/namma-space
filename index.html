    Five-minute judge path

    1. Walk round the room for a minute. Walls and furniture stop you, and you slide along them.
    2. Map (M): click a far corner and look back across the room.
    3. Find (/): type a room or item. Press Route to see the walking path round the furniture, then Follow.
    4. Reality check (C): drag the slider between a held-out photo and the live render.
    5. Tour benchmark (T): a fixed 60 s walk that prints fps, p95, splats, MB and GPU.
    6. Scale: the stats badge shows an estimate of the scale and its 68% range, from camera height. It is not a measured error. Press K and click two points to measure.
