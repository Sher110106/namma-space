The five-minute judge path

  1. Open the link and click once (the start card has a button). On the test machine the scene was on screen after ${dev_first}: a ${preview_mb} MB preview loads before the full ${sd_mb} MB scene.
  2. Walk. WASD or arrows, mouse to look. Walk into a wall: you stop and slide along it. On a phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks.
  3. Map. Press M and click the map to jump to that spot.
  4. Reality check. Press C and drag the slider between a held-out photo (never used in training) and our render at the same pose. The bar shows that pose's PSNR.
  5. Tour benchmark. Press T for a fixed walk (60 s). A result card then shows frames per second, p95 frame time, splats, download size and your GPU. Copy result puts the numbers on your clipboard: please send us yours.
  6. Scale. The stats badge (H) shows the scale estimate and its 68 % range (${scale_interval}; ${scale_status}). Press K and click two points to measure; the card shows the interval.
  7. Round 2 preview. ${places_note} Press / to search, then Jump (J), Route (G) or Follow (Space).

This is the only device we measured. Safari, Firefox, phone browsers and integrated graphics are unverified. The viewer lowers its pixel ratio on weak GPUs and falls back to the preview scene on very slow ones, so a slow machine looks softer instead of stalling. The 60 fps figure is the display cap of the test machine: it shows no dropped frames, not spare speed.

Known limits

  • Scale is an estimate with a 68 % interval; for this scene it is ${scale_status}.
  • Phone-only geometry scored below iPhone LiDAR fusion on both public rooms (F@5cm ${honka_f_ours} vs ${honka_f_lidar}; ${vr_f_ours} vs ${vr_f_lidar}).
  • Search, routes and follow are a Round 2 preview over hand-placed places. Not built: multiple floors, WebXR AR, plan registration.
  • Measured on one Apple M1 in headless Chrome. Real pointer lock, Safari, Firefox and phones were not tested; drag-to-look works where pointer lock is blocked.
  • Ceilings and close-up walls look soft in places; the reality check shows the pairs, good and poor.
