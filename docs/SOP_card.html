Namma Space capture card

One phone. Two videos. About 20 minutes of filming.

1. Choose the space 2 min

  • Pick a space with things to look at: shelves, posters, a rug, plants.
  • Avoid glass walls, big mirrors, wall-sized windows. A bare meeting room is risky: the camera path may break. Add books, bags and coats first.
  • Best: a door into a second space.
  • It will be public. Clear papers, photos, screens.

2. Set the room 5 min

  • Nobody in shot. Pets out.
  • Fans off and stopped. Curtains still.
  • Door fully open and wedged.
  • All lights on. No sun patches.
  • Cover mirrors. Screens off.

3. Set the phone

iPhone

  • Settings > Camera > Record Video: 4K, 30 fps
  • HDR Video: off. Lock Camera: on.
  • Enhanced Stabilization, Action mode, Auto FPS: off.
  • Lights flicker on screen? Use 25 fps (turn on Show PAL Formats).
  • Camera app: Video, 1×, landscape. Press and hold a mid-bright wall until AE/AF LOCK shows.

Android

  • 4K, 30 fps, main 1× lens.
  • HDR10+ off. Super Steady and other stabilisation off. Auto FPS off.
  • Lock focus and exposure (Pro video).

Setting names vary by phone. Cannot find one? Tell us the model.

  • Clean the lens. 5 GB free. Battery above 50%.
  • Film a 30 s test clip. AirDrop the original file (not WhatsApp). Wait for PASS, WARN or FAIL.

4. Take A: the room 3-4 min, one video

Chest height, landscape, both hands. Turn your body, not your wrist. One step per second. A full circle takes at least 12 s.

  1. Stand in the doorway facing in. Hold still 3 s.
  2. Outer loop: walk along the walls, 0.5 m away, camera across the room.
  3. Inner loop: 1.5 m from the walls, camera at the nearest wall, tilted slightly down so the floor line shows. At each corner tilt up to the ceiling corner for 2 s.
  4. Low pass: phone at waist height, tilted 30° down. Circle the table and big furniture.
  5. Finish where you started.

5. Take B: room plus next space 4-5 min, a separate video

  1. Repeat take A's outer loop.
  2. Walk slowly out through the door. Keep both sides in view for 3 s.
  3. Outer and inner loops in the next space.
  4. Come back through the same door, slowly. End inside the room.
Never: spin on the spot. Zoom. Switch lens. Film a bare wall from under 1 m. Walk fast. Let anyone into the shot.

6. After 3 min

  • Photo of your hand sketch: outline, door, windows, big furniture, start point.
  • Your height in cm.
  • Optional: room length and width at floor level, door width. Note how you measured.
  • Send original files by AirDrop or cable.
